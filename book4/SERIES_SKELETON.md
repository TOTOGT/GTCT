## The Arc

The series traces one construction — the dm³ contact-geometric operator chain — through
the complete Cayley-Dickson ladder and into the exceptional algebras. Each volume
corresponds to one algebraic extension. Each extension costs one property. The series
ends where the ladder ends: E₈, and the question of what lies beyond it.

```
ℝ → ℂ → ℍ → 𝕆 → [sedenions / zero divisors] → exceptional → Monster
I   II  III  IV    V        [Seg]               VI           (VII?)
                   GTCT     non-comm            Logs  Roots
```

**Property lost at each step:**
- ℝ → ℂ : ordering
- ℂ → ℍ : commutativity
- ℍ → 𝕆 : associativity
- 𝕆 → 𝕊 : division (zero divisors appear) ← Logs segment lives here
- 𝕊 → exceptional : the Cayley-Dickson ladder ends; E-series takes over
